# Week 7 - AI Prompt Log

## Prompt Variation 1

**Prompt**

Generate a travel itinerary for a trip to {destination} for {days} days within a {budget} budget.

### Example Input

Destination: Mussoorie

Days: 3

Budget: Low

### Example Output

A simple itinerary covering Mall Road, Kempty Falls and Gun Hill.

---

## Prompt Variation 2

**Prompt**

You are an expert eco-tourism travel planner.

Generate a detailed itinerary for {days} days.

Include:

- Morning
- Afternoon
- Evening
- Local food
- Budget tips
- Sustainable travel recommendations

Destination: {destination}

Budget: {budget}

Interests: {interests}

### Example Input

Destination: Mussoorie

Days: 3

Budget: Low

Interests: Trekking, Nature

### Example Output

Day-wise trekking itinerary with local food, eco-friendly travel suggestions and estimated costs.

---

## Prompt Variation 3

**Prompt**

Create a detailed travel plan for {destination}.

Include:

- Trip overview
- Daily itinerary
- Attractions
- Food
- Packing tips
- Sustainability advice
- Estimated expenses

Budget: {budget}

Interests: {interests}

### Example Input

Destination: Mussoorie

Days: 3

Budget: Low

### Example Output

Complete day-by-day itinerary with activities, meals, travel tips and budget breakdown.

---

# Best Prompt

Prompt Variation 2 produced the best results. It generated structured day-wise itineraries with clear morning, afternoon and evening activities while also including local food recommendations and sustainable travel suggestions. The responses were detailed, easy to read and closely matched the application's eco-tourism theme. It consistently produced high-quality travel plans suitable for users.
